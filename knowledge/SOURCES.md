# Medical Knowledge Sources & Licensing Registry

Every medical fact, term explanation, drug interaction, and clinical range ingested by Trinetra must be registered in this document with its formal open license, retrieval date, and canonical source identifier.

| Source Identifier | Source Name | License / Terms | Retrieval Date | Purpose in Trinetra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `src_medlineplus` | MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine) | Public Domain / Open Government Data (U.S.) | 2026-09-21 | Plain-language descriptions of conditions, diagnostic tests, and health topics. |
| `src_openfda` | openFDA Drug Product Labels | CC0 1.0 Universal / Public Domain | 2026-09-21 | Structured drug indications, contraindications, warnings, and active ingredients. |
| `src_loinc` | LOINC (Regenstrief Institute) | LOINC License (Free for commercial/non-commercial with attribution) | 2026-09-21 | Standardized laboratory test observation codes and names. |
| `src_ucum` | Unified Code for Units of Measure (UCUM) | UCUM License (Regenstrief / Open Access) | 2026-09-21 | Normalization and validation of clinical measurement units. |
| `src_icd11` | ICD-11 (World Health Organization) | WHO Terms of Use (CC BY-ND 3.0 IGO) | 2026-09-21 | Diagnostic coding categories and disease definitions. |
| `src_curated_glossary` | Trinetra Curated Indian Medical Glossary | Apache-2.0 | 2026-09-21 | Common medical abbreviations, colloquialisms, and specialist terms. |
