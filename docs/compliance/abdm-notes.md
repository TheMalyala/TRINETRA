# ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) Notes

1. **Milestone 1 (M1)**: ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) creation and verification.
2. **Milestone 2 (M2)**: Health Information Provider (HIP) — sharing diagnostic records and prescriptions into ABDM gateway.
3. **Milestone 3 (M3)**: Health Information User (HIU) — fetching consented health records from ABDM into Trinetra.
4. **Data Profiles**:
   - Diagnostic reports mapped to Indian NRCES FHIR DiagnosticReport / Observation profile.
   - Prescriptions mapped to NRCES MedicationRequest profile.
   - Identification via ABHA Address (`name@abdm`).
