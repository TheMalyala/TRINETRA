# NMC Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) Compliance Notes

1. **Practitioner Identification**:
   - All doctors onboarding to Trinetra must submit their State Medical Council or National Medical Commission (NMC) registration certificate and number.
   - Doctor profiles display an explicit "Unverified" badge until vetted by an administrator.
2. **Prescription Issuing Constraints (Deferred - D7)**:
   - Automated electronic prescription generation is deferred pending clinical and legal review.
   - Schedule X medicines (e.g. narcotics, psychotropics) are prohibited from telemedicine issuance.
   - Current MVP provides **Advice Logging** (diet, lifestyle, diagnostic tests ordered, referral notes) and **Prescription Upload/Explainer** only.
3. **Emergency Disclaimers**:
   - Explicit disclaimers ("Not for emergencies. For medical emergencies call 112 or 108 immediately") are prominently displayed at the entry to chat and appointment screens.
