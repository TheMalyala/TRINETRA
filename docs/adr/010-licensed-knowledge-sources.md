# ADR 010: Exclusively Licensed and Grounded Medical Knowledge Sources

## Status
Accepted

## Context
AI models that rely on ungrounded pre-trained weights for medical drug interactions, dosages, and clinical guidelines frequently hallucinate outdated or incorrect medical claims. Scraping commercial drug databases introduces severe licensing and copyright liabilities.

## Decision
All medical facts must be ingested and retrieved from open-access, authoritative public sources registered with provenance in `knowledge/SOURCES.md`:
1. **MedlinePlus / MedlinePlus Connect**: Plain-language condition and drug summaries.
2. **openFDA**: Drug labeling, boxed warnings, and active ingredients.
3. **LOINC & UCUM**: Laboratory codes and clinical measurement units.
4. **ICD-11**: Disease classifications from the WHO API.
5. Curated Indian brand-to-generic mappings reviewed by licensed pharmacists.

Every medical assertion generated by the assistant must carry an explicit citation to a chunk in the knowledge store (`src_*`).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Strict regulatory compliance, full provenance, zero reliance on LLM parametric memory.
- **Negative**: Requires maintaining an ingestion pipeline and license registry.
