# Microcopy & Voice Guidelines

## Microcopy Voice
**Terse. Calm. Unflinching.**  
Avoid filler words, emotional hype, or alarming tones. Deliver verified clinical context with utmost precision.

### Standard Exemplars
- **Scanner Confirmation**:  
  *"Report scanned. 14 values extracted. Confirm each value before saving."*
- **Biomarker Trends**:  
  *"HbA1c is trending downward over 8 months (7.2% -> 6.4%)."*
- **Out-of-Range Observation**:  
  *"▲ Above printed reference range (14.2 g/dL; range 12.0–15.0). Ask your doctor why."*
- **Clinical Non-Diagnosis Disclaimer**:  
  *"This is an explanation, not a medical diagnosis. Please discuss these values with your doctor."*
- **Emergency Intercept**:  
  *"Urgent. Potential emergency symptoms detected. Call 112 (National Emergency) or 108 (Ambulance) immediately."*
- **Advice Ledger Tamper Check**:  
  *"Audit chain verified. 28 entries intact with cryptographic proof."*
